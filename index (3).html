How it works

A quick action can start an alert when you need support.

1

Shake your phone 4–5 times

The app is designed to recognise a deliberate shake gesture when it is set up and ready to use.

2

An alert is triggered

The app is designed to start your personal alert flow, based on your chosen settings and permissions.

3

Your trusted people are notified

Your chosen parents or trusted contacts and, where supported, local police can be notified.

Important: alerts depend on your setup, device permissions, network connection, and available local support. Bravewomen.in does not promise emergency response in every location.
